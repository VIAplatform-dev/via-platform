FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you might want to know about running your store on VYA. Still stuck? We're one message away.

What exactly is VYA?
VYA is a purpose-built platform for one-of-one commerce, the resale, vintage, and consignment stores where every item exists only once. It handles listing, cross-posting, offers, messaging, payments, and shipping in one place, so you can spend less time on admin and more time sourcing.
Do I need a website already?
Not at all. You can launch a full storefront in minutes with no code and no setup fees. And if you already have a website, VYA integrates every page and all your listings with a single click, so nothing gets left behind.
Where do my items get listed?
Your own storefront, the VYA marketplace, and every outside channel that matters, like eBay, Depop, Vestiaire, Instagram, and Google. Every item is a single living record that stays in sync everywhere and clears automatically the moment it sells, so you never double-sell.
How does listing and pricing work?
Snap a photo and text it in like you would to a friend. VYA identifies the piece, writes the title and description, and suggests a price based on hundreds of recent comparable sales. You are always in control of the final number before it goes live.
Does VYA handle payments and shipping?
Yes. Checkout, payments, payouts, and shipping labels are all built in, so you never leave VYA to get paid or send an order. Consignor splits are tracked and settled automatically.
Can I use VYA for consignment?
Absolutely. Consignment is built in, not bolted on. Track every consignor, item, and split in one place, and let payouts settle on their own, no spreadsheets required.
Is my inventory and data secure?
Your store data is yours. We use industry-standard encryption and never sell your information. You can export your catalog and leave at any time.
How much does it cost?
We are finalizing pricing for launch. Book a demo and we'll walk you through the details and early-access terms.
