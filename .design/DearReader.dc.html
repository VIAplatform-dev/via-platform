A note

I started this because I kept finding things worth keeping and running out of room for them.

I look for pieces that are well made and likely to last. That means I list fewer things than I could, and I would rather do that than fill the shop with items I do not believe in.

Everything is washed and repaired where it needs it. I measure each piece flat and photograph it on a person, so you can see how it actually sits rather than how it looks on a hanger.

If something does not fit, send it back. If you are looking for something specific, tell me and I will keep an eye out.
