What this is

A small collection of secondhand clothing, added to a few pieces at a time.

Pieces are chosen for how they are made rather than for the label. There is no seasonal turnover and no sale rail, and when something sells it is not replaced with something similar.

Everything is photographed in daylight against the same background and measured flat. Any wear is described in the listing and shown in the photographs.

On condition

Everything is graded on the same five-point scale. The grade appears on every listing with a note on what informed it: a repaired seam, a missing button, a mark that did not come out.

Photographs are not retouched, and everything is shot in the same light so pieces can be compared against each other.
